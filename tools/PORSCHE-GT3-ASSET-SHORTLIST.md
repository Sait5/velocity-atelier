# Porsche 911 GT3: model and audio shortlist

Research date: 2026-10-08. No assets installed, purchased or downloaded. Entries checked against publisher descriptions and published metadata; audio has not been auditioned and model archives have not been inspected. Project currently labels GT3 as a 2025 vehicle.

   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/2025-porsche-911-gt3-992-2-1503f8d08f154ec7ab52f95a99c55134
   Free; 308.4k triangles, 174.4k vertices. CC BY-NC-SA. Publisher explicitly describes a CSR2-derived model and credits GM25. Exact-year candidate for a noncommercial prototype, not a cleared commercial asset. Interior and separate animated parts unverified. Live page returned 403; metadata came from indexed publisher page.

   https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/porsche-911-gt3-78d5c47ab2554c2592b7e499179a0792

## Coherent engine library (paid)

Porsche 992 GT3, Sounding Sweet / Sweet SFX
https://www.sweetsfx.com/product/porsche-992-gt3/?currency=GBP
Published price range £79–£199 excluding VAT. Short & Sweet has 52 WAV files at 96kHz/24-bit, approximately 1.46 GB uncompressed. Standard has 233 files, approximately 8.42 GB. Check selected edition and license at checkout; nothing purchased.

Publisher's Short & Sweet file list:
https://www.sweetsfx.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/SSFX-P992GT3_Short_and_Sweet_FileList.pdf

Coverage from one road-going 4.0L flat-six GT3:
- Ignition, steady idle and engine shutdown: Ignition On Idle Off, 30 seconds, external/engine/exhaust/interior perspectives.
- Slow general driving, acceleration, deceleration, coasting and shifts: up to 2:47 onboard.
- Medium/fast general driving: 1:05 and 0:50 onboard engine/exhaust; interior perspectives also included.
- Horn and external pass-bys.

This is raw recording material, not a ready seamless game loop. Sustained engine playback still requires selecting stable sections and checking joins. Separate door and tire effects below complete the interaction set; no seat sound requested.

## Selection conclusion

For a coherent road-going GT3 engine across startup, acceleration, coasting and shutdown, the paid Sounding Sweet library is the strongest verified-content candidate. For zero budget, the links above provide audition candidates but do not constitute a complete cleared GT3 engine library. Do not silently substitute racecar, unknown car or generic recordings as exact GT3 sounds. Seat audio is unnecessary; cabin engine playback can use the exterior engine at a lower level instead of layering another engine recording.
